# Addis Hands design direction

## Tone (Oura)
- Canvas: warm cream `#f7f1e8`
- Ink: near-black warm `#1c1b1a`
- Accents: clay `#6b443d`, gold `#af751b`, olive `#5c6f5d`
- Display: light editorial serif (Newsreader)
- Body: quiet grotesque (Figtree)

## Product photography (Nike / Apple)
- Hero is edge-to-edge media with brand lockup overlaid — no inset logo card in hero
- Product/company cards are image-first; borders and shadows stay minimal
- Hover scale on media; ken-burns on hero
- Spec panels use light hairline rings, not heavy card chrome

## Buttons
- Soft 8px radius (not pill)
- Primary: espresso fill
- Secondary: hairline outline on cream / white stroke on dark hero

## Do not
- Purple gradients
- Multi-layer decorative shadows
- Floating badges over hero media
- Crowding the first viewport with stats or listings
